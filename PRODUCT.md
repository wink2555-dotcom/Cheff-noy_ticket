# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS with Tailwind CSS

## Users

Students, teachers, parents (pre-ordering lunch/meal tickets for children), school canteen stall vendors, and school canteen admins.

## Product Purpose

An intuitive, fast, and responsive School Food Ticket & Pre-order Booking Web Application ("ระบบจองตั๋วอาหารโรงเรียน") allowing users to browse school canteen stalls/menus, pre-order meal coupons/tickets for specific dates and lunch slots, receive digital QR food tickets, and enable food stall vendors to scan and redeem tickets efficiently.

## Positioning

A lightweight, zero-bloat school meal ticket booking portal that eliminates long canteen queues by allowing students and parents to pre-book meal tickets with QR vouchers for instant pickup at canteen stalls.

## Operating Context

Web browser environment (smartphones and desktop viewports), used by students/parents to pre-order food tickets before lunch, and by canteen vendors at stall counters to scan QR codes on smartphones or printed passes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities: Canteen stall & food menu catalog with category filtering (Rice dishes, Noodles, Drinks & Desserts, Snacks), date/meal time slot selector, food ticket checkout, digital QR Meal Ticket Pass generator, "My Food Tickets" management, and Stall Vendor QR Code Scanner & order fulfillment dashboard.
- Technical Constraints: Plain HTML5, Tailwind CSS, modular Vanilla JavaScript, localStorage for persistence/mock data.
- Language/Localization: Thai interface ("ระบบจองตั๋วอาหารโรงเรียน").

## Brand Commitments

- Warm, appetizing, friendly educational canteen UI design.
- Clear visual status badges for unused vs redeemed meal tickets.

## Evidence on Hand

- Greenfield workspace at project root `d:\บูรณาการ\เว็ฟแอป`.

## Product Principles

1. **Queue-Free Dining**: Pre-order meal tickets to skip order lines at school canteen stalls.
2. **Instant QR Meal Voucher**: Generate clear QR coupons instantly redeemable at stall counters.
3. **Daily Meal Slots**: Easy date and lunch break slot selection (พักเที่ยง รอบ 1 / รอบ 2).
4. **Mobile First**: Fast smartphone access for students on break.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Clear visual status badges (พร้อมใช้งาน, สแกนแล้ว), high contrast menu cards, price totals, keyboard navigation.
